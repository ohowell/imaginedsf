Jean Jacques Vioget, “Plan of Yerba Buena,” United States District Court (California: Northern District), Land case 424, 1839.

Source: [Online Archive of California](https://oac.cdlib.org/ark:/13030/hb70000713/?brand=oac4)

The first survey of the pueblo of Yerba Buena, 1839, by Jean Jacques Vioget, a Mexican national of Swiss descent and a tavern keeper. The map does not align perfectly with the modern grid. In 1846 the pueblo hired Jasper O’Farrell resurvey the town, and he found that “Vioget’s North was eleven degrees too far east.”

See Geoffrey P. Mawn, “Framework for Destiny: San Francisco, 1847,” *California Historical Quarterly*, Vol. 51, No. 2 (Summer, 1972), [https://doi.org/10.2307/25157373](https://doi.org/10.2307/25157373)

## Imagined San Francisco

This project collects the most significant maps in the history of San Francisco and makes them accessible to the public, as well as to researchers. Imagined San Francisco includes not only maps of the city as it was, but also unrealized urban plans that show the city as it might have been. Maps and plans are organized chronologically by era. Each entry is accompanied by a brief essay that situates the object in its historic context. Each map also features an opacity slider that enables visitors to fade maps in and out over one another, and over the modern and historic basemaps. (The console for the basemaps appears in the lower right of the homepage.) Many maps are also accompanied by pin layers that display historic photographs and architectural renderings.
